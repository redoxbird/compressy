Are JPG and JPEG different?

No. Same format — JPG is three letters, JPEG is four. Compressy treats both as JPEG and converts to WebP the same way. Use either page.

Can I batch .jpg and .jpeg together?

Yes. Drop a folder — both extensions are found and converted to WebP together, keeping subfolders.
