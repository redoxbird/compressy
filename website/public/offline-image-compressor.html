    Does Compressy really work offline?

    Yes. The desktop app runs on your PC with the network off — all compression is local, nothing is uploaded, no account needed.

    What formats can I compress offline?

    JPG, PNG, WebP and AVIF. Keep the original format or convert to WebP/AVIF for the smallest files.

    Can I batch a whole folder?

    Yes. Drop any folder — Compressy finds images in subfolders, keeps your structure and writes results alongside.

    Is there a file size limit?

    No. Since processing is local, there is no upload limit — only your disk.
