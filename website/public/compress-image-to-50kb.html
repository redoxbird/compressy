    Can you guarantee exactly 50KB?

    No. The smallest possible size depends on dimensions, format, and content. Compressy lets you try combinations locally and preview the result before saving. If 50KB is required, try lower quality, smaller dimensions, or WebP/AVIF.

    What settings hit 50KB most often?

    For avatars/icons: 512×512, WebP/AVIF, quality 70–75. For photos at 50KB, you’ll likely need both resize and WebP/AVIF.
