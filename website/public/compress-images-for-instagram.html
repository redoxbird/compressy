    What size should I upload to Instagram?

    1080px on the long edge. Anything bigger gets re-encoded (and softened); Compressy resizes to 1080px before upload.

    Will this make my feed sharper?

    Yes. Uploading at 1080px instead of 4000px avoids Instagram's downscale blur — your feed stays crisp on phones and tablets.

    Can I batch a whole shoot?

    Yes. Drop the folder — posts, stories and carousel images all compress together, backups to /backup.

    Do I need to upload?

    No. All compression is local. No upload, no account.
