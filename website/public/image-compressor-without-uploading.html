Is any part of my image uploaded?

No. The desktop app compresses entirely on your PC. No image data leaves your machine — no upload, no telemetry on your files.

Do I need internet at all?

No. Once installed, Compressy works fully offline. Download, then unplug — it still compresses.

Can I still send the result to someone?

Yes. You just export the smaller file and share it however you like — the sharing is yours, not the compressor's.

Is there a file limit without uploading?

No. Local processing has no upload cap — only your disk space.
