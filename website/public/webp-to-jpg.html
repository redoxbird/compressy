Why is JPG larger than WebP?

WebP compresses better at the same quality — 25–35% smaller. Converting back to JPG re-expands the file; you do it only for compatibility.

Will I lose transparency?

Yes. JPG has no alpha — transparent areas become white. Keep WebP or PNG if you need alpha.

Can I batch a folder?

Yes. Drop a folder, Compressy converts every WebP inside, keeps subfolders.

Do I need to upload?

No. All conversion is local. No upload, no account.
