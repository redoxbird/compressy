Is WebP lossy?

WebP can be lossy or lossless. For JPG → WebP you pick lossy quality 40–95 — 75–85 is visually near-lossless and still 30% smaller.

Can I batch a folder?

Yes. Drop a folder, Compressy converts every JPG inside, keeps subfolders, writes WebP alongside or to a chosen output.

What quality should I pick?

85 for photos you keep, 75 for web delivery, 90+ for near-lossless archival. Try 75–85 and compare side-by-side.

Do I need to upload?

No. JPG → WebP is local. No server, no account.
