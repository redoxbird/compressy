Same workflow.

Compressy is designed to make compression feel like a normal Windows utility — not another website you have to upload files to. Compress individual images or work through a whole folder without sending anything over the internet.

1Step one
Select

Choose a folder or select images in. The file list shows size, type, and dimensions — with checkboxes for exactly what you want to compress.

2Step two
Compress

Set quality, engine, and output format. Run it locally — 2, 4, or 8 threads — and watch per-file savings appear on the right.

3Step three
Done.

Export a CSV, open the folder, or overwrite in place with backups to a backup folder. No upload step in between.

Formats

Built for the formats you actually use.

JPEG, PNG, WebP, AVIF and more. Compressy handles common image formats while giving you control over how aggressively your images are compressed.

JPGmozjpeg

Photographs and exports. Quality slider 40–95 with balanced, fast, and maximum engines.

PNGoxipng

Screenshots, UI, and transparency. Lossless toggle keeps PNG/WebP bit-exact when you need it.

WEBPmodern

Smaller web delivery. Keep original or convert — WebP as output for the whole batch.

AVIFnext-gen

Maximum density when size matters most. AVIF output without leaving the app.
