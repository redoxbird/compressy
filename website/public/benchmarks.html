    Why only 3 configs?

    These three cover the common paths: JPG recompression, PNG→WebP conversion, and JPG→WebP conversion, all at quality 85 balanced. The app supports the full matrix — quality 40–95, three engines, keep or convert output.

    Why is png→webp 84.5% vs jpg→jpg 57.8%?

    PNG is largely uncompressed source, so converting to WebP shrinks it dramatically. JPG recompression at the same quality still saves ~58% thanks to mozjpeg plus metadata stripping.

    Can I reproduce these numbers?

    Yes. Download the app, point it at your own folder, quality 85 balanced — you should see savings in the same ballpark. Your images never leave your PC.
