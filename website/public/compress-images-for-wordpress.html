    Do I need a plugin?

    No. Compress locally on Windows, then upload the small files. No plugin, no server-side processing, no upload to a third party.

    Will this speed up my WordPress site?

    Yes. Smaller images improve LCP and reduce page weight. Compress your media library before upload or re-upload the optimized folder.

    Can I batch the whole uploads folder?

    Yes. Drop wp-content/uploads — Compressy finds images in subfolders, keeps structure, writes WebP/AVIF alongside.

    Do I need to upload?

    No. All compression is local. No upload, no account.
