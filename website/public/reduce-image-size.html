How much can I reduce image size?

Typically 20–40% at Quality 85 balanced, more if you lower quality, resize, or convert to WebP/AVIF. Per-file preview shows it before you save.

Will reducing size reduce quality?

You control it. Lower quality = smaller file, higher = more fidelity. Preview the trade-off per file.

Can I reduce a whole folder?

Yes. Select a folder, filter by type, batch on 2/4/8 threads. No upload.

Do I need to upload?

No. All reduction is local. Your images never leave your disk.
