When AVIF wins over WebP

30–50% smaller than JPG, 15–25% smaller than WebP — at a slower encode you pay once.

For web hero images and portfolios where every kilobyte matters, JPG → AVIF saves the most. WebP is faster and more compatible; AVIF is smallest. Compressy lets you output both in one batch — JPG → WebP + JPG → AVIF — and compare side-by-side before you ship. All encode stays on your disk.
