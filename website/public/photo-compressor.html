Will my photos lose quality?

You control it. Quality 85 is visually near-lossless for most photos. Preview per-file before you save.

Can I keep EXIF?

Yes. Keep or strip EXIF/ICC per batch. For web, strip; for archive, keep.

Can I do a whole shoot at once?

Yes. Select the shoot folder, filter to JPG/JPEG, batch on 2/4/8 threads. Backups to /backup.

Do I need to upload?

No. All photo compression is local. No upload, no account.
