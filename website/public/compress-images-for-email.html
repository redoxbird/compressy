    What is the email image limit?

    Gmail caps attachments around 24KB per inline image, Outlook 20MB total. Shrink inline images well under the cap; Compressy shows the size before you attach.

    Will WebP work in email?

    Most modern clients render WebP, but keep JPG for maximum compatibility. Compressy exports both so you can pick per audience.

    Can I batch all my attachments?

    Yes. Drop the folder — Compressy compresses every image and shows per-file savings before you attach.

    Do I need to upload?

    No. All compression is local. No upload, no account.
